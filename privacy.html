Privacy Policy for Bolify

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Bolify ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application Bolify (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Audio & Voice Data (Microphone Permission)

When you participate in speaking exercises or engage in two-way voice conversations with Ava (our AI conversation partner), the App accesses your device microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO).

  • Purpose: Audio input is converted to text locally or securely processed to evaluate your pronunciation, fluency, and conversational grammar, and to generate conversational AI responses.
  • Retention: We do not sell, rent, or trade your voice data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.

B. Account & Profile Information

  • Account Credentials: If you register or authenticate via Supabase Auth (email or OAuth), we store your email address and unique user identifier.
  • Learning Profile: Your speaking proficiency level (A1 to C2), user avatar, target learning goals, native language preference, daily streaks, and completed lesson days.

C. Learning Progress & Analytics

We collect your fluency scores, words spoken, speaking duration, and lesson completion history to present your personalized learning statistics dashboard.

D. Financial Transactions

Gem packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details. We receive only the purchase confirmation, product name and order reference so we can add gems to your account.

E. Content You Report

If you report a response from Ava, we store the reported text and your reason so we can review it and improve safety.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Provide real-time AI speech evaluation, pronunciation feedback, and natural voice dialogues.
  • Maintain your speaking statistics, streak counters, and daily roadmap progression.
  • Synchronize your learning profile across devices using encrypted cloud storage (Supabase).
  • Verify purchases and prevent fraudulent activity.

3. Data Storage & Security

All network communications between Bolify, Supabase, and AI inference endpoints are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS (SSL) protocols.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

The App integrates with trusted, privacy-compliant third-party providers:

  • Supabase: Cloud database and secure authentication.
  • Google Play Services: App distribution, security, and Android framework capabilities.
  • Google Play Billing: Payment processing for gem packs.
  • Google (Gemini API), Groq and LiveKit: Your speech transcripts and messages are sent to these AI providers to generate Ava's replies and feedback. They process the data on our behalf and we do not allow them to use it for advertising.

5. Children's Privacy

Bolify does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately to have the data removed.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Reset Progress: You can reset your learning progress and statistics at any time inside the App's Profile tab.
  • Delete in the app: Open Profile, then tap Delete account. Your account, profile, progress, call history and bookmarks are deleted immediately and permanently.
  • Delete by email: Email manishjoshi182003@gmail.com from your account email with the subject "Delete my Bolify account". We delete it within 7 days. Details: manish182003.github.io/delete-account.html.
  • What we keep: Google keeps its own purchase records as required by law. Anonymous usage logs that cannot identify you may be kept for up to 90 days.
